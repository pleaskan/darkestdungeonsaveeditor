Your save files

The editor runs entirely in your browser. The files in your profile folder are read and rewritten on your computer; they are never sent to a server, neither ours nor anyone else's. The ads shown on the page have no access to them.

In Chrome or Edge, your browser may remember the last folder you opened and offer it again. This setting is handled by the browser itself and stays on your device.

Google AdSense advertising

The site is free and funded by a few ads provided by Google Ireland Limited (Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland).

  • On your first visit from the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent window appears. It is provided by Google, certified and compliant with the IAB TCF. You can accept, refuse or choose purpose by purpose; refusing is as easy as accepting.
  • Until you accept, no advertising cookie is set: the site applies “denied” consent by default.
  • If you refuse, ads may still appear, but they are limited or non-personalized (chosen based on the page content and your approximate location, not your browsing history).
  • If you accept, Google and its partners may use cookies to personalize ads based on your visits to this site and others, limit how often they repeat and measure their effectiveness.
  • The list of advertising partners and their purposes can be viewed at any time in the consent window.

Learn more: how Google uses data from partner sites, advertising at Google and My Ad Center to adjust ads linked to your Google account.

Managing your consent

Your rights

Under the GDPR and the French Data Protection Act (loi Informatique et Libertés), you have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to object to and restrict its processing, and to withdraw your consent. Write to contact@pleaskanworkshop.fr; we reply within one month. For advertising data, since Google is responsible for its own processing, you can also exercise your rights directly with Google through its dedicated form.

You can lodge a complaint with the CNIL (French data protection authority).
