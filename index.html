      Where is my save?

      Steam

      C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\userdata\<your Steam ID>\262060\remote\profile_0

      The number after userdata depends on your account. If there are several, pick the one that contains the 262060 folder.

      GOG, Epic and DRM-free version

      C:\Users\<you>\Documents\Darkest\profile_0

      Each save slot has its own folder: profile_0 is the first, profile_1 the second, and so on.

      Frequently asked questions

      My changes disappear when I launch the game

      It is almost always Steam Cloud restoring the online version. In Steam: right-click Darkest Dungeon → Properties → General → untick “Keep games saves in the Steam Cloud”. Also close the game before saving.

      The “Choose folder” button does nothing

      Writing directly to a folder works in desktop Chrome, Edge, Opera and Brave. In Firefox or Safari, use compatibility mode: the editor downloads the three edited files, which you then copy over the originals.

      How do I undo my changes?

      Every time you save, the original files are copied to profile_X/editor_backup/date-time/ (older versions of the editor used sauvegarde_editeur/). Copy them back into the profile_X folder to undo. The game also keeps its own copies in profile_X/backup.

      Which files are modified?

      Only persist.estate.json (gold, heirlooms, trinkets), persist.roster.json (heroes) and persist.game.json (estate name). Despite the .json extension, they are binary files specific to the game.

      The trinkets I added don't show up

      They go to the estate inventory, visible from the Hamlet (Stage Coach window or a hero's sheet). Trinkets from a DLC you don't own may not appear; remove them with the editor.

      How many quirks can a hero have?

      The game supports at most five positive and five negative quirks, not counting diseases. The editor blocks adding more.

      Are my files sent anywhere?

      No. Reading and writing happen locally in your browser. The site shows ads, described in the privacy policy, but they have no access to your files.

      Does it work with Darkest Dungeon II?

      No, only with the first Darkest Dungeon (and its DLCs: Crimson Court, Shieldbreaker, Color of Madness, Musketeer, Flagellant).
